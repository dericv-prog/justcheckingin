A little note from the creator

Why I made this

I made #justcheckingin for World Mental Health Day. In my work in mental health, I've seen how hard it can be to start a conversation — especially when we're not used to checking in, or when work and life keep us apart. This is a small way to make reaching out a little easier (and maybe a little less awkward).
